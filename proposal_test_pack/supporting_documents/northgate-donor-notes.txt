Notes from discovery call — Northgate Youth Foundation

- Foundation has approximately 640 donors on file, roughly 200 of whom gave more than once in the last 3 years.
- Repeat-donation rate has fallen for two consecutive years, currently around 22%.
- Board specifically asked for a report they can review quarterly without needing staff to compile it by hand.
- Some donor records still only exist on paper index cards from before 2019 and will need manual entry.
- Executive director mentioned a hard budget ceiling for this project of $16,000 — flag if scope discussion approaches that.
