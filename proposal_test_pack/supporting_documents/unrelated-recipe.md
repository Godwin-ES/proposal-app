Grandma's Sunday Pot Roast

Ingredients: 3lb chuck roast, 4 carrots, 2 onions, 6 red potatoes, 2 cups beef stock, rosemary, thyme, salt,
pepper.

Sear the roast on all sides in a hot dutch oven. Remove, sauté the onions until soft, then return the roast,
add stock and vegetables, cover, and braise at 300°F for about 3.5 hours until fork-tender. Rest 15 minutes
before slicing.
