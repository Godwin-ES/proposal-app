Discovery call notes — Cobalt Street Music Academy

- 22 instructors, roughly 310 active students across piano, guitar, voice, and drums.
- Billing is currently done by one part-time admin who reconstructs each family's lesson count from a group text
  thread at the end of every month — Julian said this alone takes her nearly 3 full days.
- Make-up lessons for cancellations are a frequent source of billing disputes; whatever is built needs to track
  make-up lessons distinctly from regular ones, not just lump them together.
- Julian wants parents to be able to request a reschedule themselves within some rule (e.g. 24 hours notice)
  rather than everything going through the admin.
