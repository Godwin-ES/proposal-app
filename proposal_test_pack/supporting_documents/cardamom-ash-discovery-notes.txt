Discovery call notes — Cardamom & Ash Coffee Roasters

Lucas runs wholesale accounts for 34 cafes across two metro areas. Order volume: roughly 210 individual
weekly orders during a normal week, rising to near 260 around holidays.

Two recurring problems he wants solved, in his own words:
- "At least once a month someone reads a handwritten order wrong and we roast the wrong bean for a whole
  account's week."
- "Cafes call in when they haven't heard back, and half the time it's because the order got buried in a
  group text, not because we ignored them."

He specifically does not want cafes required to create an account with a password — he said repeatedly that
adding friction to ordering is worse than the current error rate, and a simple order-submission link per cafe
would be preferable to a full login system.

Internal note (Lucas asked this stay out of anything shown to competitors, but it's fine for the proposal
itself): the roasting schedule the portal would feed into is a proprietary batch-sequencing spreadsheet built
by a former employee — any integration needs to output into that exact spreadsheet format, not replace it.
