Sales team notes — Meridian Solar Solutions

Current stages leads pass through: Inquiry -> Site Assessment -> Quote Sent -> Contract Signed -> Permitting ->
Install Scheduled -> Installed. Each stage currently lives in a different person's spreadsheet:

- Inquiry and Site Assessment: shared sheet owned by the intake coordinator.
- Quote Sent and Contract Signed: separate sheet owned by sales.
- Permitting and Install Scheduled: separate sheet owned by operations.

Carlos estimated roughly 15% of leads currently go cold specifically during the handoff between sales and
operations, because nothing prompts operations to pick up a newly signed contract. He wants that handoff to be
the first thing this fixes, even before the full pipeline view is built out.
